About Me

I hold an MSc in Business Analytics (MSBA) from the NUS Business School, and before that I studied Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Shiv Nadar University in India.

My professional experience began at Bain & Company in New Delhi, where I worked on cost benchmarking and procurement strategy for large industrial clients. In Singapore, I joined Arta Finance as a data analyst, building portfolio reporting and automating analytics for its wealth management business. My MSBA capstone was with EY Singapore's technology consulting (Data & AI) practice, where I led UAT for a GenAI platform on the Government Commercial Cloud and coordinated four teams through defect resolution ahead of launch.

Since July 2025, I have been a Founders' Associate for Strategy and Product at an early-stage consumer credit venture. I benchmarked more than twenty fintech products, identified an underserved gap in the market, and developed the positioning thesis now underpinning its institutional fundraising. My work also spans market sizing, competitive intelligence, product strategy, and fundraising across South and Southeast Asia.

I hold the AFP® designation in Singapore and was also a finalist in the NUS-GURU Network AI Innovation Challenge 2025.
